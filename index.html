The Preamble

A word before we begin

Raksha Bandhan is supposed to be simple: a thread around your wrist, a promise between two people, and perhaps a little sibling chaos along the way. But some bonds deserve more than just a ritual. So, before you proceed, there are four little trials waiting for you. Think of them as the terms and conditions of being my sister — a lifetime of teasing, unconditional support, shared secrets, ridiculous arguments, and memories I wouldn’t trade for anything.

Pass them, fail them, or simply enjoy the journey — there’s no real score to keep. Because at the end of it all, there’s something waiting for you that matters far more than any gift: a few words from my heart, meant only for you. 🤍

Dear Sis,

Some people become special quietly — through countless little moments, shared laughter, silly arguments, secrets, and memories that somehow become the most precious ones. You’ve become one of those people in my life, and I don’t say that nearly enough. Thank you for being my constant, for understanding the things I don’t always say, for putting up with my chaos, and for making ordinary moments feel a little more cherishable.

This Rakhi is more than just a thread around your wrist. It is a small reminder of something much greater — that no matter how much life changes, you’ll always have a place in my heart and someone standing beside you.

May your life be filled with serendipity, quiet happiness, and everything beautiful you deserve. And whenever you need me, you already know — I’ve got you.

With all my love,
Your Brother - Sarthak
